1. Purpose

This terms and conditions document outlines the rules, eligibility requirements, referral procedures, GDPR obligations, reward payments, and compliance standards applicable to participants in the BHE Uni Ambassador, Referral & Rewards Programme.

The purpose of the programme is to encourage genuine referrals and support prospective students seeking access to higher education opportunities within the United Kingdom.

4. Qualified Lead Requirements

A lead will only be considered valid where:

  • The individual has provided consent to be contacted by BHE Uni
  • Full and accurate contact details have been supplied
  • The individual has expressed a genuine interest in higher education opportunities
  • The lead has not previously been registered within BHE Uni systems
  • The lead has not been submitted by another ambassador or referral source
  • The lead satisfies minimum programme eligibility requirements

BHE Uni reserves the right to determine whether a lead is valid.

5. Duplicate Lead

Duplicate referrals are not eligible for rewards.

The CRM system records:

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Mobile Number
  • Submission Date and Time

The first valid submission recorded within the CRM system shall be recognised as the official referral source.

Any subsequent submissions relating to the same individual shall be rejected.

Where disputes arise, BHE Uni reserves the right to determine referral ownership at its sole discretion.

6. Fraud Prevention & Acceptable Conduct

The following activities are strictly prohibited:

  • Submitting fake or fabricated leads
  • Self-referrals
  • Creating duplicate accounts
  • Using false or misleading information
  • Manipulating referral data
  • Claiming rewards dishonestly
  • Purchasing leads
  • Sending unsolicited bulk messages or spam communications
  • Collecting personal information without consent
  • Using automated tools to generate referrals

BHE Uni reserves the right to:

  • Suspend or terminate participant accounts
  • Cancel pending rewards
  • Recover rewards paid in error
  • Remove participants from the programme
  • Pursue legal action where appropriate

8. Reward Payment Terms and Conditions

Lead Referral Rewards

Payment will be issued following:

  • Lead validation
  • Completion of compliance checks
  • Confirmation that the lead is eligible for UK home fee
  • Confirmation that the lead has a genuine interest in studying
  • Confirmation that the lead meets Student Finance England eligibility requirements

Payment Terms and conditions

  • A minimum reward balance of ৳50 is required for payment by bank transfer to the Ambassador's bank account.
  • Where the reward balance is less than ৳50, the reward may be issued as a BHE Uni digital voucher/gift card instead.

Expected Payment Timeframe:Payments will normally be processed within 3–5 working days once all validation and compliance checks have been completed.

Enrolment Rewards

Enrolment Rewards are payable only in respect of enrolments onto higher education courses at BHE Uni partner institutions. Enrolment onto any course offered by Digital Marketing School ('DMS') does not qualify for an Enrolment Reward. Where a referred individual who has enrolled with DMS subsequently enrols onto a higher education course through a BHE Uni partner institution, the Enrolment Reward may become payable in respect of that higher education enrolment only, subject to all other conditions in this Section.

Rewards become payable only when:

  • The student has successfully enrolled
  • University enrolment has been verified
  • Student finance England funding has been approved and received by the university partner
  • The university partner has paid BHE Uni for the referral
  • No refund or chargeback risk applies
  • The student remains actively enrolled as confirmed by the institution
  • Has not been withdrawn, cancelled, deferred, or rejected
  • Remains eligible under the institutional agreement for commission payment

Expected Payment Timeframe:

  • Payment will be issued only once BHE Uni has received payment from the university partner, following all the above confirmations.

The reward will be paid in 2 instalments as follows:

  • First instalment will be paid in 4–6 months after enrolment
  • Second Instalment will be paid in 9–12 months after enrolment

The ambassador should make a claim from our ambassador portal and previous referral agents should send an invoice through invoice.bheuni.io link to claim the invoice or send an invoice directly to our referral representative.
